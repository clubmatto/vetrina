# 📣 Marketing Writing

Read `writing.md` first. This file adds rules for launch posts, social posts,
product pages, email, and landing copy.

## 🎯 Reader And Job

The reader is a prospect with a problem, not a peer reading documentation. They
are deciding whether to care, believe, and act. Give them one reason to care,
one reason to believe, and one thing to do.

## 🗣️ Voice

Confident, concrete, human. Enthusiasm is allowed; empty hype is not. Write like
a founder explaining why they built the thing, not like a press release.

- Use "we" and "you". Contractions are normal.
- Keep paragraphs short. One idea per line where the channel is short.
- Vary the rhythm. A one-line paragraph can land a point.
- Personality is welcome. Corporate polish is not.

## 🎯 One Message Per Piece

If you cannot state the message in one sentence, the piece is not ready. Every
section should support that sentence or be cut.

## 🪝 The Hook

The first line earns the second. Name the change, the problem, or the number.

- "Guido 1.4 is out, and it is one app now."
- "A month of coding-agent work on DeepSeek cost us $41.58."
- "Gran Turismo 7 was the easy one."

Avoid:

- "We are excited to announce..."
- "In today's fast-moving landscape..."
- A history of the company before the news.
- A feature list before the reader knows why it exists.

## 🧱 Body Shape

1. **Hook**: what changed or what is possible now.
2. **Tension**: the problem, the gap, or the workaround.
3. **Promise**: what the reader can do instead.
4. **Proof**: a demo, a number, a command, a screenshot, or a quote.
5. **Caveat**: what is not ready, who it is not for, or what it costs.
6. **CTA**: one action.

Short posts can drop the caveat if there is nothing honest to add. Long posts
should not.

## ✅ Claims

- Use specific, checkable claims. "Runs in 10.5s on one million rows" beats
  "blazing fast".
- Never invent numbers, users, benchmarks, quotes, or social proof.
- Say "free", "closed beta", "invite-only", or "early access" only when true.
- Name the limitation before the reader finds it.
- Do not use "revolutionary", "game-changing", "cutting-edge", "seamless",
  "best-in-class", "world-class", "unlock", "empower", or "supercharge" unless
  you are quoting someone or proving the claim in the next sentence.
- No fake urgency. No countdowns that reset.

## 🔗 Calls To Action

One primary action per piece. Do not stack links.

| Channel      | Close with                                     |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------------------- |
| LinkedIn     | `Read it here: <url>` or `Check it out: <url>` |
| X / Twitter  | The bare URL on its own line                   |
| Instagram    | `link in bio`                                  |
| Product page | One primary button, then exact availability    |
| Email        | One link, one action                           |

Use active link text. "Read the launch post" is better than "click here".

## 😀 Emoji

Optional. One pair maximum, or a single anchor at the end. Emoji mark the kind
of news, they do not replace a claim.

- 🚀 launch or release
- 📢 article, series, or announcement
- 🏎️ or 🏁 racing and Guido
- 🤔 curiosity or a question
- ✨ highlight
- 🔧 fix

Use emoji in the first line for launches and announcements on social channels.
Do not put emoji in every heading or every line.

## 📱 Channel Matrix

| Channel      | Length                          | Shape                                               | Link                       |
| ------------ | ------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| LinkedIn     | 100 to 200 words usual          | Hook, context, proof, caveat, close                 | `Read it here:` at the end |
| X / Twitter  | 280 characters max, aim for 200 | Thread parts numbered; one idea per part            | Bare URL on its own line   |
| Instagram    | Short caption                   | Visual first; 5 hashtags max; 3:4 image             | `link in bio`              |
| Reddit       | As long as needed               | TL;DR first; disclose affiliation; answer questions | Link in context            |
| Product Hunt | Short                           | What, who, why now; detail in the first comment     | Demo or site link          |
| Product page | Scannable                       | Tagline, benefits, proof, availability, CTA         | One primary CTA            |
| Email        | Short                           | Specific subject, one message, one CTA              | One link                   |

X is LinkedIn trimmed. Same hook, cut to essentials.

## 🚀 Launch Shape

1. What is new.
2. Why it matters to the reader.
3. The proof: demo, number, or concrete capability.
4. The caveat: beta, platform, price, or limitation.
5. The CTA.

## 🛍️ Product Page Shape

1. **Tagline**: one promise in three to eight words.
2. **Intro**: the problem and the promise in two or three sentences.
3. **Benefit blocks**: a benefit heading, one to three sentences, and proof.
4. **Demo**: video, screenshot, or terminal recording.
5. **Availability**: price, beta status, platforms, exact terms.
6. **CTA**: one primary action.

Lead with the benefit. The feature is the proof, not the headline.

## 🚫 Anti-Patterns

- A feature dump with no problem.
- A launch post that never says what changed.
- "We are thrilled to announce" with no concrete reason.
- Vague social proof: "loved by developers".
- A CTA that asks for three things at once.
- Emoji as a substitute for a claim.

## 🧪 Examples

```markdown
<!-- ✅ GOOD: concrete, short, one idea -->

Guido 1.4 is out. It reads sim telemetry from shared memory on your PC and
carries it to your phone over your network.

<!-- ❌ BAD: hype, no information -->

We are thrilled to announce the revolutionary launch of Guido 1.4, a
game-changing solution for the modern sim racer.
```

```markdown
<!-- ✅ GOOD: number and comparison -->

A month of coding-agent work on DeepSeek cost us $41.58. The same tokens at
published frontier rates would cost between $878 and $7,486.

<!-- ❌ BAD: adjective without proof -->

DeepSeek is incredibly cost-effective.
```

```markdown
<!-- ✅ GOOD: honest caveat -->

Guido is still a closed beta, and mostly Gran Turismo rigs so far. If you race
Le Mans Ultimate or an Assetto Corsa, you are the rig we are looking for.

<!-- ❌ BAD: overclaim -->

Guido is available for everyone today.
```

## 🔍 Self-Check

- Does the first line earn the second?
- Is there one message?
- Is every claim provable?
- Did I name the caveat?
- Is there one CTA?
- Does it fit the channel limits?
- Would a customer forward this?
