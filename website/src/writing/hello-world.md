We're Club Matto — a software bottega.

What's a bottega? It's an Italian workshop where craftspeople make things by hand, with care and intention. We build tools the same way: small teams, sharp focus, and a stubborn refusal to ship things that don't spark joy.

Our products — [Kube Shield](/products/kube-shield/), [DB Diff](/products/db-diff/), [FakeData](/products/fakedata/), [Bookmarks](/products/bookmarks/), [Guido](/products/guido/), and [Matto Bar](/products/matto-bar/) — each solve a real problem that we've felt ourselves. We don't build for a hypothetical market; we build for our own workflows, our own frustrations, our own "someone should fix this" moments, and then share the result with anyone who shares the pain.

Beyond products, we're open to light consulting. If you have a project that needs careful thinking, a tricky system that needs untangling, or a team that could use a seasoned pair of hands for a stretch — [reach out](/contact/). We keep it small and sustainable.

This is just the first post. More to come.
