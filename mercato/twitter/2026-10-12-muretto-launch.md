### 1

🚀 Muretto 1.0 is out 🚀

Guido could only read Gran Turismo 7, because the console broadcasts its
telemetry. Le Mans Ultimate and the Assetto Corsa games keep theirs in shared
memory on the PC, out of a phone's reach.

### 2

Muretto reads that memory and relays it to Guido over your network.

Install it, launch the game, drive. No manual tinkering whatsoever.

### 3

It lives in the tray and starts itself when you log on, so it is already running
when you launch a sim. No admin rights, and small enough not to cost you frames.

### 4

Muretto is free: https://matto.club/muretto

Guido is a closed beta, and mostly Gran Turismo rigs so far. Race Le Mans
Ultimate or an Assetto Corsa? You are the rig we are looking for:
https://matto.club/products/guido/#beta-testing-program
