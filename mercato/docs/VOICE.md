# Club Matto voice

This is the local brand layer. Read `.agents/rules/writing.md` first, then the
profile for the task: `writing-editorial.md` for blog posts, and
`writing-marketing.md` for launches and social. This file only records what is
specific to Club Matto.

## Who we sound like

A small software bottega: craftspeople who build tools they use, explain them
plainly, and admit what is not ready. Direct, warm, a little playful. Never
corporate, never a press release.

## Person

- "We" for the company.
- "I" for personal stories and origin posts.
- "You" for the reader.
- Contractions are normal.
- Dry humor is welcome when it does not obscure the point.

## Rhythm

- Short paragraphs, often one or two sentences.
- Vary sentence length. A short sentence after a long one lands.
- Use concrete numbers, product names, and commands.
- Avoid stacked exclamation marks and stacked emoji.

## Emoji

Optional, one pair maximum, always as an anchor:

- 🚀 launch
- 📢 article or series announcement
- 🏎️ or 🏁 Guido and racing
- 🤔 curiosity or a question
- ✨ highlight
- 🔧 fix

Do not use emoji in every line. Do not use emoji to replace a claim.

## Claims

- Quote real numbers from our work.
- Say "closed beta", "free during early access", or "invite-only" when true.
- Name the limitation before the reader finds it.
- No invented users, benchmarks, or quotes.
- No fake urgency.

## Calls to action

- Articles: `Read it here: <url>`
- Products: `Check it out: <url>`, or the product name plus the URL.
- Installs: `Install: <command>`
- X: a bare URL on its own line.
- LinkedIn: one link at the end.
- Instagram: `link in bio`.
- Product pages: one primary CTA.

## Product names

Use these spellings:

Club Matto, Vetrina, FakeData, FakeData Pro, ai-kit, DB Diff, DB Diff Pro,
Guido, What's my local AI?

Do not invent short forms.

## Examples

Good:

- [The October launch post](../posts/2026-10-12-muretto-launch.md): concrete problem,
  product proof, honest beta caveat, two clear links.
- [The tool tradeoff](../posts/2026-10-13-the-tool-tradeoff.md): one idea, one
  number, one link.
- [Meet Guido](../../website/src/writing/meet-guido.md): scene, tension,
  product, caveat, CTA.

Avoid:

- A feature list with no problem.
- "We are thrilled to announce" without a concrete reason.
- A launch post that never says what changed.
- A post that hides the beta status or the price.

## Maintenance

Update this file when a product launches, a channel changes, or the team agrees
on a new phrase. Keep the shared rules generic; keep the brand quirks here.
