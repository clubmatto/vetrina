🚀 We just launched What's my local AI? 🚀

Everyone tells you local models are the way to go so we made it easier to figure
out which ones your machine can actually run. LLM specs are intimidating: VRAM,
tokens, Q4, unified memory...

So we built a tiny browser tool to help you with that. Open the page and we
will: detect your GPU, RAM, VRAM, and OS right in the browser.

Tweak the specs and see what a bigger laptop would unlock before you buy one.

No install, free, open source: https://whatsmylocal.ai

Give it a try and let us know what it detects on your setup 👇
