At Club Matto, DeepSeek is the primary token provider for agentic programming.

We love it because it's fast, the output quality is very high, and is very,
very cheap. Sometimes, it has actually felt too cheap!

So, we asked ourselves: what would it cost on the so-called "frontier" models?

A month of coding-agent work on DeepSeek cost us $41.58.

The same 6.2B tokens at published frontier rates:

GPT-6.1 Sol: $878 (21x)
Claude Fable: $2,844 (68x)
GPT-6 Astra: $7,486 (180x)

DeepSeek may not be as smart as frontier models but a 21x to 180x premium is
quite hard to justify.
