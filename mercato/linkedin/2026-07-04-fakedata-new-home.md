FakeData, my little CLI data generator, has a new home!

I've archived lucapette/fakedata and the project now lives at Club Matto part of Vetrina (https://lnkd.in/dQKnqhgf), our open-source monorepo (Italian for "shop window". Cheeky we know so give it a star!).

The rewrite brings significant UI/UX improvements:

- Column spec syntax: name:generator[:options]
- Country-specific generators: phone_it, city_de, state_us
- Deterministic seeding
- and many other little things

Docs: https://lnkd.in/dcPd5vSu

Thanks to everyone who used and contributed to this project over the years!
