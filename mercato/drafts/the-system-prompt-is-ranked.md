## LinkedIn

A system prompt is not magic text. On modern models it's a ranked instruction channel: root, system, developer, user. Lower ranks cannot override a contradicting statement in a higher rank.

That rank is why your harness can configure the agent the same way for every user, and guard what you type. And it's why the prompt carries three things in almost every harness: where it is, your rules, and what it learned.

Read it here: https://matto.club/writing/how-coding-agents-work/the-system-prompt/
