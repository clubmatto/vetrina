This is Guido, in three parts. 1/3: the left dial.

Two tyre temperatures and a fuel gauge. The numbers a race engineer
watches so you don't have to: which tyre is working, and how far the
tank will take you.

Guido reads them off your sim, live, over your own network.
At 250 km/h you don't do fuel maths in your head. That's its job.
