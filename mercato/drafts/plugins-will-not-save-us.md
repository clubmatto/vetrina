## LinkedIn

Plugins will not save us.

There is a healthy market of plugins and add-ons around every coding harness,
and most of it is good work. We do not believe it can fix the core problem:
the improvements land at the edges, because as long as the loop stays the same
and everything has to pass through the model, the ratio between what you spend
and what you get barely moves.

Two examples from our own week to week. Session handover: we wanted to pass a
complete session to another person, and in OpenCode the only way to make it
work was clunky and error-prone. Codebase indexing: the best answer today is
still grep and the filesystem, because harnesses see context management as a
conversation-length problem rather than an information problem.

Both are from our closing article on the future of coding agents, where we
argue the next generation of harnesses has to rethink the loop as one tool
among many, not the only one.
