## LinkedIn

📢 The local AI cost calculator is live on whatsmylocal.ai. 📢

"Is it cheaper to run this myself?" has two answers, so the calculator has two
modes. I own it: electricity only, and at the defaults (350 W, two hours a
day, $0.25/kWh) that is $5.25 a month. I am buying it: the rig amortized over
its life, and a $2,000 machine across three years lands near $61 a month
all-in.

Then the fork. The same page prices your usage against GPT-5.5 and DeepSeek
V4 Pro, per month and per million output tokens: at today's catalog snapshot
that is $50 per million for GPT-5.5 and $4.62 for DeepSeek, assuming four
input tokens per output. An order of magnitude apart, and the weekly catalog
refresh moves those prices too. At the default rig, one month of local is
worth about 1.2M output tokens on GPT-5.5, or 13M on DeepSeek.

The page also computes the payback months for each, the usage crossover, and
a 12-month cumulative chart with both crossings. When the cloud wins, it says
so.

Check it out: https://whatsmylocal.ai/cost/
