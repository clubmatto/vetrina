🏎️ Guido 1.3 is out: Assetto Corsa EVO joins the beta.

EVO keeps its telemetry in shared memory on the PC. Muretto 1.1 reads it and relays it to your phone, the same

#### 2/3

The fuel gauge reads EVO's own percentage, and the laps-in-fuel call works because the sim publishes the track
position we need for it.

Guido is a closed beta on Android, with the iPhone build on TestFlight.

Update both apps together: Guido 1.3 and Muretto 1.1 speak a new protocol, and older builds are not supported.

## LinkedIn

🏎️ Guido 1.3 is out, and Assetto Corsa EVO joins the beta 🏎️

Gran Turismo 7 was the easy one: the console broadcasts its telemetry. PC sims keep theirs in shared memory,
where a phone on the network cannot reach it. That is what Muretto is for, and 1.1 learns EVO.

EVO publishes its telemetry the way Le Mans Ultimate and the Assetto Corsa games do, so from Guido's side this
is one more decoder and no new plumbing. Speed, gear, revs, tyre temperatures and the aid flags land on the
dashboard you already know.

One thing worth saying plainly, because you will find it otherwise.

1.3 changes the protocol between the app and Muretto. Update both together: an old Muretto and a new Guido will
not talk, and the app now says so instead of showing an empty dashboard.

EVO is also the newest sim we read, and the thinnest: the dashboard, fuel, laps and lap times are in, and the
tyre detail the other Kunos games carry is not, yet.

Closed beta and free, on Android and TestFlight. If you race EVO, you are the rig we are looking for.
