## LinkedIn

🏎️ Guido 1.4 is out, and it is one app now 🏎️

Gran Turismo 7 was the easy one: the console broadcasts its telemetry. PC sims keep theirs in shared memory,
where a phone on the network cannot reach it. Since the first release that job belonged to a second program you
had to install, update and remember.

Not any more. Guido runs on the PC too: the same app, the same dashboard, and it reads Le Mans Ultimate and the
Assetto Corsa games from shared memory and carries them to your phone over your own network. One product, one
version, one download, and no "update both" to get wrong.

The same release adds Assetto Corsa EVO to the beta. Speed, gear, revs, tyre temperatures, fuel and laps, with
the fuel gauge reading EVO's own percentage and the laps-in-fuel call working off the track position the sim
publishes. EVO is still the thinnest sim we read: the tyre detail the other Kunos games carry is not in yet.

Closed beta and free, on Android and TestFlight. If you race EVO, you are the rig we are looking for.

https://matto.club/guido
