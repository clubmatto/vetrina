🚀 Today we're releasing Guido: your personal race engineer for Gran Turismo 7 🚀

Every race driver has someone on the radio — a person who reads the telemetry
and does the math mid-race so the driver can just drive. Sim racers got
dashboards instead: numbers on a second screen you have to read at 250 km/h.
We thought the spare phone on our rigs deserved better, so we built the
co-driver we were missing.

Guido turns that phone into a live race dashboard — shift lights, gear,
speed, tire temperatures, assist status — and, more importantly, into a voice
engineer:

- he learns how much fuel *your* car burns per lap and calls "box this lap"
  before you run dry
- you hold push-to-talk and ask: fuel left? laps to go? what's the advice?
- he answers out loud while you keep your eyes on the track

And everything stays home: telemetry never leaves your network, speech
recognition runs on your device, no cloud, no ads, no analytics.

The dashboard and push-to-talk are free. A free account unlocks hands-free
voice activation (Pro).

Guido is out today on Android, with the iPhone release right behind it — it's
already in App Store review. It's our first Kotlin Multiplatform product: one
codebase driving all three platforms.
