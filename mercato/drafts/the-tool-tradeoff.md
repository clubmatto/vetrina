## LinkedIn

📢 How coding agents work: tools 📢

Tools are where an agent's capability is enforced, and the biggest cost its
author controls: every declaration is text you pay for on every turn.

We read ten open-source agents and found that no two agree on how much surface to
expose. The spread runs from a single shell to about thirty tools. The two ends
do not just cost different amounts, they buy different things, and picking
between them is a balance rather than a best practice.
