# 1/5

🚀 We just released Guido: your personal race engineer for Gran Turismo 7 🚀

Not another second-screen dashboard — a co-driver. A thread on what he does 👇

# 2/5

The dashboard: a spare phone becomes a full-screen race display. 16-LED shift
lights, gear and speed at peripheral-vision size, per-tire temps, downshift
cues, assist status.

Zero setup: Guido finds your PS5 on the network by himself.

# 3/5

The engineer: Guido learns how much fuel your car actually burns per lap, and
when the tank stops covering the distance he says it out loud: "Box this lap."

Hold push-to-talk to ask — fuel left? laps to go? — he answers while you
drive.

# 4/5

Private by design: telemetry stays on your network, speech recognition runs
on your device. No cloud, no ads, no analytics.

Dashboard + push-to-talk are free. A free account unlocks hands-free voice
(Pro).

# 5/5

Our first Kotlin Multiplatform product: one codebase, Android today, iPhone
right behind it (in App Store review now), desktop from the same code.

Android beta: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=club.matto.guido
