## LinkedIn

📢 whatsmylocal.ai v2 is coming, and it is a power-up, not a rewrite. 📢

v1 did one thing: it looked at your machine and told you which local AI models
fit. No install, no upload, your browser reading your own hardware. That idea
stays exactly where it was, and nothing got thrown away.

v2 turns that one answer into four: how far off you are when a model doesn't
fit, how fast it runs, what it costs to run, and what to run it on.

The power-up starts with speed. Every model in the catalog now carries a tok/s
estimate, with the band coming from the llmspeed bandwidth model, and a
simulated terminal that streams at that speed and restarts the moment your
specs change. Behind it, the catalog refreshes weekly, so the answers track
the models rather than last month's snapshot.

The countdown: the gap lands Wednesday, cost on Thursday, and the full v2 on
Product Hunt next Monday.

No install, free, open source. Give it a try: https://whatsmylocal.ai
