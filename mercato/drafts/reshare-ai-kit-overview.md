## LinkedIn

One install, and every agent in the repo shares the same rulebook.

AI Kit syncs rules, skills and commands into your project. It detects your
languages, handles monorepos, and needs zero config: run the installer and
your agents pick up the same rulebook, whatever they are.

Since v0.1.0 the rulebook comes in four rulesets: a universal core for voice
and structure, plus technical, editorial, and marketing profiles. Docs read
like docs, posts read like posts.

The overview page carries the demo, and the demo does the talking.

Check it out: https://matto.club/vetrina/ai-kit/
