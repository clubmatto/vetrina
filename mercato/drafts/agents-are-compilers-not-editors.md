## LinkedIn

Coding agents are compilers, not editors.

Think about the move from assembly to high-level languages. Compilers raised
the level of abstraction, and a generation of tools grew around the new level:
syntax highlighting, navigation, refactoring. The IDE exists because more
people expressed intent and let a program expand it for the machine.

Agentic coding raised the abstraction level again, and the harness is the
compiler. What has not arrived is the IDE-class tooling for the new level.
Every action, from a sweeping refactor to renaming a symbol, passes through
the model as a prompt. In an IDE a rename costs nothing. In a harness it
invokes an LLM and spends tokens, every single time.

Closing the gap is the argument of our closing article: treating the loop as
one tool among many, why plugins cannot fix the structure, and the UX patterns
that have not been invented yet.
