## LinkedIn

💡 We read the source of ten open-source coding agents so you don't have to.

Where they agree, they agree completely:

- The loop is nearly identical, and small. A few dozen lines.
- Context management is the same trick everywhere: drop what's cheap, keep the recent tail, summarize only when you must.
- A tool is a name, a schema, a function.

The hard problems are at the edges, not the loop. That is where the ten split, and where your daily experience gets decided.
