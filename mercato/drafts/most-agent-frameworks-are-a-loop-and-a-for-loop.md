## LinkedIn

Most "agent frameworks" are a loop with a for-loop inside.

We read ten open-source coding agents for a comparative analysis, and behind
every framework sits the same few-dozen-line loop: model call, tool result,
repeat. A system prompt, some tools, a context window, a way to compact it.
The differences are in the decisions, not the skeleton.

That's the take: the loop is shared. When you adopt a framework, you're
paying for everything around it — and when you build your own, you're not
building as much as you think.
