## LinkedIn

🚀 Muretto 1.0 is out 🚀

Gran Turismo 7 was the easy one. The console broadcasts telemetry and Guido
picks it up. Le Mans Ultimate and the Assetto Corsa games keep theirs in shared
memory on the PC, where a phone on the network cannot reach it.

Muretto reads that memory and relays it to Guido over your own network. It is a
Windows app that lives in the tray: install it, launch your sim, drive. No tinkering with configuration files or
exotic .dll copy-pasting.

It starts itself when you log on, asks for no admin rights, and stays small
enough not to cost you frames.

Muretto is free, and so is Guido's dashboard. Guido itself is still a closed
beta, and it has mostly been Gran Turismo rigs so far. If you race Le Mans
Ultimate or an Assetto Corsa, you are the rig we are looking for.

Muretto: https://matto.club/muretto
