This is Guido, in three parts. 2/3: the rev bar. And the pitch. 🚀

Guido turns a spare phone into a race engineer for Gran Turismo 7,
Le Mans Ultimate and Assetto Corsa. The shift lights, gear and
speed are the bonus. The real feature: Guido learns how much fuel
your car actually burns, calls "box this lap" before you run dry,
and answers out loud when you ask. Fuel left? Laps to go? Hold the
button, it answers while you drive.

Zero setup: it finds your rig on the network by itself. No cloud,
no ads, no analytics: your races never leave your Wi-Fi.

The dashboard and push-to-talk are free. Beta open on Android and
iPhone, link in bio. 🏁
