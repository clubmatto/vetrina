### First comment

We built v1 in August because "can my machine run this?" was a guessing game.
It answered fit. The next questions were always the same: how far off am I, how
fast will it actually run, is the hardware worth it, and what should I buy?

v2 answers those four.

The gap is the one I use most. A no without a number is useless. Every model
that does not fit now shows the GB short, sorted by the closest miss. On a 16
GB M1, 46 models fit and 46 do not; the closest are about 2.5 GB short.

Speed is an estimate, not a benchmark. It uses the llmspeed bandwidth model for
a single device at 8k context, and shows a band because real numbers move with
the engine, drivers, and thermals. The terminal streams at that speed so you 
you can feel it.

The cost page compares the same tokens, not the same quality. Local is a fixed
cost with a low marginal cost; GPT-5.5 and DeepSeek are marginal. The
calculator has two modes, payback, the usage crossover, and a 12-month chart
with the crossings marked.

The setups are the last piece. Four machines that clear 20 tok/s at the low
end, with the model and speed computed from the catalog: Mac mini M4, MacBook
Pro M4 Pro, RTX 4060 Ti, and RTX 4090. Vendor links and prices are public; no
affiliate money.

Everything runs in the browser. No install, no account, nothing uploaded. 90+
Q4 models, Ollama and LM Studio, open source.

What does it detect on your machine? I would love to see the specs and the 
pick.
