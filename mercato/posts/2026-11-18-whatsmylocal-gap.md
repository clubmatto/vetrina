## LinkedIn

A no is only useful when it comes with a number.

Our model lists used to tell you a model doesn't fit and stopped there. You 
were left guessing whether you are 1 GB away or 20, and whether another machine is worth it.

Now What's my local AI? shows the gap for every model that doesn't fit, sorted by the smallest gap 💅
