## Description

Stop guessing which local models your machine can actually run. Open What's my
local AI and it detects your GPU, RAM, VRAM, and OS right in the browser, then
tells you exactly which models fit your hardware — Ollama or LM Studio — with a
copy-paste run command for each.

Everything runs client-side. Nothing leaves your machine.

Also includes:

- a simulator to see what a bigger GPU or more RAM would unlock
- a performance vs. resources slider
- filters and search across 90+ models
